# v0.01 Flaw Inventory — logical & realism flaws (evidence-based)

Probed from live code, 2026-09-03. Each flaw cites its evidence.

## Logical flaws (rules that break their own intent)

### L1. Prices can never fall (one-directional price discovery)
- **Evidence:** engine.py:826 — `_validate_bid` rejects any bid price < cost baseline.
- **Why it's wrong:** the floor was meant to stop *predatory dumping*, but it also forbids *honest deflation*. A good nobody wants keeps its price forever; efficiency gains never reach consumers; surplus gluts can't clear by price. The 'market' only ratchets up.
- **Fix idea:** allow listings below floor only when the seller has >N days of unsold stock (honest clearance), or let baselines themselves drift down when a good persistently under-sells.

### L2. Wages mint money unconditionally (demand-side inflation built in)
- **Evidence:** engine.py:571 — wages are minted by hours worked, no link to production actually sold.
- **Why it's wrong:** money supply grows with every worked hour even if the work produces goods nobody buys. The mint is backed by *labor*, not by *value delivered*. Long-term this is a hidden tax on everyone (inflation) and the root of 'idle labor mint' pressure.
- **Fix idea:** mint wages from the coop's treasury when possible (transfer), mint only the shortfall (auto-credit from society), or tie mint to cleared sales.

### L3. Births mint 500 credits each (population = money printer)
- **Evidence:** demographics.py:66-69 — each birth mints a 500cr stake.
- **Why it's wrong:** collective stake should be a *transfer* from society's pool, not new money. Fast growth = silent inflation.
- **Fix idea:** pay the stake from the surplus pool (a real cost of welcoming new members).

### L4. Wealth tax ignores inventory (tax-free hideout)
- **Evidence:** engine.py:1554+ — tax base is `state.balances` only.
- **Why it's wrong:** buy goods → hold them forever (nothing decays) → zero wealth tax. The tax fights inequality in money only.
- **Fix idea:** tax base = balance + inventory valued at cost baseline.

### L5. First-come-first-served clearing is structurally unfair by default
- **Evidence:** engine.py:997 — fair rotation is votable but default OFF.
- **Why it's wrong:** whoever bids earliest wins scarcity, rewarding bot speed (tick-order luck), not need or price. In a market-socialist ethos, distribution by queue-jumping is a design smell.
- **Fix idea:** need-weighted or lottery-clearing as default; FCFS as the opt-in.

## Realism flaws

### R1. Nothing ever spoils (perfect pantry world)
- **Evidence:** no decay/rot anywhere in src/.
- **Consequence:** hoarding has zero carrying cost — a flaw amplifier for L4. Real economies are shaped by perishability.
- **Fix idea:** per-good shelf-life (bread 5 ticks, tools never), consumed FIFO from inventory.

### R2. Labor is homogeneous and instantly redeployable
- **Evidence:** JOIN_COOP/LEAVE_COOP move a citizen between *any* coops with zero friction; everyone works any recipe.
- **Why it's wrong:** a miner is not a baker. Skill-free labor teleportation makes the economy absurdly flexible.
- **Fix idea:** per-citizen skill vector that grows with time-in-trade; joining a new trade starts at reduced hours until trained.

### R3. No credit money — the credit union lends but nothing leverages
- **Evidence:** credit.py moves existing credits; no interest, no deposits competing, no maturity mismatch.
- **Why it's wrong (as realism):** real economies' credit volume exceeds base money; ours can never. Fine for v0.01, but financial depth is still decorative.

### R4. Demand signals are myopic
- **Evidence:** bots bid on today's need + short buffer; unmet streaks look back 5-20 ticks.
- **Why it's wrong:** no seasons, no foresight, no buffer-building against known shock cycles (droughts recur!). Producers never invest ahead of predictable demand.

### R5. One clearing price per good per tick (no geography, no quality)
- known from the geography gap, but compounded by: all units of a good are identical, so 'quality competition' cannot exist; the only competitive axis is price, which is floored (L1) — competition is nearly meaningless as implemented.

## Priority for v0.02
1. **L2 (wage minting)** — it's the deepest monetary inconsistency.
2. **L4+R1 (tax base + decay)** — closes the hoarding arbitrage.
3. **L1 (price floor)** — make deflation possible, keep anti-predator intent.
4. **L3 (birth stakes from pool)** — small change, big consistency gain.
5. **R2 (skills)** — the biggest realism unlock for the game.
